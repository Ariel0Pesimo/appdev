 My Favorite Movie: Three Idiots

Three Idiots** is a 2009 Indian comedy-drama film directed by Rajkumar Hirani. It is based on the novel *Five Point Someone* by Chetan Bhagat.

 Main Characters
- Rancho (played by Aamir Khan)
- Farhan (played by R. Madhavan)
- Raju (played by Sharman Joshi)
- Virus (played by Boman Irani)
- Pia (played by Kareena Kapoor)

 Memorable Quotes
> "All is well."  
> "Pursue excellence, and success will follow."

 Why I Love It
- It's funny, inspiring, and heartwarming.
- It teaches us to follow our passion and not just chase grades.
- The story is very relatable, especially for students.
